🚀 Want Continuous 30 FPS Live Video? (1-Min Setup) ▼

Mobile Chrome protects your camera on local Wi-Fi. You can grant permanent permission with 1 simple setting:

  1. Open a new tab in Chrome on this phone and go to:
    chrome://flags/#unsafely-treat-insecure-origin-as-secure
  2. In the text box, paste your PlantCare Hub origin:
    http://...
  3. Change dropdown to Enabled and tap Relaunch.
  4. Refresh this page — continuous 60 FPS video streaming will activate automatically!
📹 Prefer Using Free Android "IP Webcam" App? ▼

For a permanent 24/7 dedicated surveillance camera:

  1. Install the free IP Webcam app from Google Play Store on your phone.
  2. Open the app, scroll to the bottom, and tap Start server.
  3. The app will display an IP address on screen (e.g. http://192.168.0.X:8080).
  4. In PlantCare Pro Studio on your computer, click 📱 Connect 4 Phones and paste that URL into the IP Webcam box!
